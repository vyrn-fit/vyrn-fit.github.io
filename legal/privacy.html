Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Vyrn (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. It is designed to align with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws.

1. Who we are

Vyrn is a fitness web application that provides equipment-free workouts and competitive challenges. Contact: privacy@vyrn-fit.github.io (or via the GitHub repository issues).

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, password (hashed by our auth provider), optional display name.
  • Usage data: workouts completed, challenge times/scores, streaks, and preferences you set (e.g. fitness goal).
  • Technical data: browser type, device type, approximate region (from IP), and basic analytics needed to run the service.
  • Local storage: some progress may be stored on your device for offline use.

We do not currently collect precise GPS, heart-rate, biometric, camera, or microphone data. We do not sell personal data.

3. How we use data

  • To provide and improve the app (auth, leaderboards, progress).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To communicate important service updates (not marketing spam).

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

We process data based on: (a) performance of a contract (providing the service you signed up for), (b) legitimate interests (security, product improvement), and (c) consent where required (e.g. optional marketing).

5. Third parties

We use Supabase for authentication and database hosting. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policy and data processing terms. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Retention

We keep account and workout data while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time. Backups may retain data for a limited period after deletion.

7. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Export your data (data portability).
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to certain processing and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
  • California residents: rights under CCPA/CPRA including “Do Not Sell or Share” (we do not sell data).

To exercise rights, contact us using the details above. We will respond within applicable legal timeframes.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and secure auth. No method is 100% secure; use a strong unique password.

9. Children

Vyrn is not directed at children under 16 (or higher age where required). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, additional notice in the app.

11. Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository or email privacy@vyrn-fit.github.io.
