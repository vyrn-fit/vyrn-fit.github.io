## Free vs Pro — one-pager

### What is Vyrn?
A mobile-ready fitness app for **equipment-free** training. Open it in the browser or install as a PWA. Workouts are designed for **home, office, and outdoor** spaces — no dumbbells, no machines, no excuses.

**Tagline:** *Show up. Put in the work.*

---

### Free — $0
Built to be useful on day one.

| Included | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Core workout library** | Office Reset, Desk Posture Fix, Home Full Body, Core Focus, Morning Wake-Up, Mobility Flow, Playground Circuit, Playground Easy, Micro 4, Travel/Hotel |
| **Guided form** | Setup → Move → Finish on every exercise |
| **Voice coach** | Male or female trainer voice + optional SFX |
| **Timers & reps** | Time caps + rep targets where it makes sense |
| **History** | Track sessions on this device (sign in to sync) |
| **Guest mode** | Try instantly — no account required |

**Best for:** Getting started, desk breaks, travel days, building a streak.

---

### Pro — $7 / month
Everything in Free, plus the full training library and room to push harder.

| Included | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Full library** | Afternoon Energy, Home HIIT Blast, Legs & Glutes, Upper Body Pump, Park Power, Express 6, and all Free workouts |
| **Unlimited history** | Session log and comparisons across days |
| **Cloud sync** | Sign in so progress follows you across devices |
| **Friends challenges** | Create/join invite-code races, leaderboard (honor system) |
| **Early features** | First access as community and coaching expand |

**Best for:** People who train most days and want variety + accountability.

*Demo note: in-app Upgrade currently unlocks Pro for testing; real billing can be wired later (Stripe, etc.).*

### Positioning (for partners / press)
- **Not** a gym tracker. **Not** a copy of race-style event brands.  
- Original bodyweight programming for real environments: desk, living room, playground.  
- Social layer is **friends-first** (invite codes), not public leaderboard toxicity.  
- Built as a **PWA first** — share a link, no app-store friction for beta.

### Contact / feedback
Ship feedback via your usual channel (DM, email, Discord). Early users shape the roadmap: nutrition phase, deeper anti-cheat for public races, and native wrappers later.
